# Visual Design Philosophy: Lithic Glass & Tactile Whisper

**Movement Name**: *Lithic Glass* (*Tactile Whisper*)  
**Domain**: QırımKey iOS / iPadOS Keyboard Extension & Host Application Visual Architecture  
**Date**: 2026-11-08  

---

## 1. Space, Form, and Negative Breathing Room

Every square millimeter of a mobile keyboard is contested territory between human biomechanics and visual noise. *Lithic Glass* resolves this tension by treating the keyboard not as a dense grid of mechanical buttons, but as a quiet optical instrument suspended over living glass. Form is defined by restraint: keycaps shed heavy drop shadows and opaque boxes in favor of whisper-thin specular top rims, continuous squircle curvature (`cornerRadius = 8.5 pt..14.0 pt`), and generous typographic breathing room. When the user explores the **Typographic Air (Borderless)** mode, the rectangular cages around alphabetic characters dissolve completely—leaving pure, high-contrast letterforms floating in calibrated spatial harmony, while only the structural anchor keys (`Shift`, `Delete`, `123`, `Space`, `Return`) retain translucent glass bodies to ground peripheral thumb memory. This is spatial orchestration that looks as though it was labored over with painstaking care by a master typographer and industrial designer.

## 2. Color, Materiality, and Cultural Chromatic Resonance

Color in *Lithic Glass* is never decorative wash; it is an optical material system rooted in Crimean landscape and architectural heritage. Beneath the default Apple iOS 26/27 translucent surface lies a curated spectrum of cultural materiality: the sun-warmed oolitic limestone and filigree gold (`#D49B1A` / `#FFD700`) of **Bağçasaray**, the deep bioluminescent slate and sky-blue (`#1295D8` → `#0B72B5`) of **Qara Deñiz Midnight**, the warm unbleached rag paper of **Aqmescit Minimalist Paper**, and the sage-and-amber horizon of **Steppe Dawn**. Translucent layers interact through subtle alpha calibration (`0.17` to `0.95`), allowing host wallpaper light to refract softly through keycap edges without ever compromising WCAG AAA glyph legibility. Every chromatic token is the product of deep expertise and countless hours of perceptual calibration across Light and Dark environments.

## 3. Scale, Typographic Rhythm, and Diacritic Reverence

A 12-column Crimean Tatar keyboard (`Qırımtatarca`, `Къырымтатарджа`, `Кʼиримтатарджа`) carries unique orthographic architecture—diacritics (`ğ`, `ñ`, `ş`, `ç`, `ö`, `ü`, `â`), digraphs (`къ`, `гъ`, `нъ`, `дж`), and the Ukrainian smart apostrophe (`ʼ`). Rather than crowding primary glyphs with heavy secondary flick legends, *Lithic Glass* establishes a strict two-tier typographic rhythm: primary letterforms command the optical center (`21.5 pt..25.5 pt` regular/medium), while secondary flick characters recede to a quiet whisper (`22%` resting opacity) that blooms to `42%` only during an active vertical flick or touch proximity. In the top toolbar, the daily wisdom phrase (`Atalar sözünde altın bar.`) floats completely unplattered (`Color.clear`) in native suggestion typography, reserving the stadium pill (`14.0 pt`) strictly for active autocorrect commitments.

## 4. Kinetic Choreography and Bio-Responsive Motion

Motion in *Lithic Glass* obeys physical inertia and biological cadence rather than arbitrary timers. At the center of the spacebar rests the 9×5 geometric **Taraq Tamğa**—the living pulse of QırımKey. Driven by phase-continuous CoreAnimation (`0.25 -> 0.52` opacity, `0.975 -> 1.045` scale), its breathing accelerates proportionally with human typing exertion (`15..75 WPM` up to `2.9x`), cools down with smooth exponential inertia like a runner catching their breath, and settles into tranquil stillness after 60 seconds of silence. Across the keyplane, the **QırımKey Swipe v2** comet ribbon glides with fold-free quadratic Bezier filleting and Chaikin smoothing, subtly illuminating turning-point anchor keys ($\Delta\theta \ge 22^\circ$) with a magnetic rim glow and releasing a micro-pulse across the Tamğa when a word crystallizes into text.

## 5. Editorial Structure in the Host Application ([HomeView.swift](file:///Users/love/Documents/Documents%20-%20MacBook%20Air%20-%20%D0%92%D1%96%D1%82%D0%B0/GitHub/Keyboard/QTatar/Demo/Demo/Views/HomeView.swift))

In the companion application, the generic SaaS card stack is replaced by an **Editorial Bento Architecture**. Information is encoded through structural proportion, tactile spring-slide switchers (`[QT | УК | EN]`), stacked bottom Cyrillic and Ukrainian rows (`settings.cyrillic.statusRow` and `settings.ukrainianCyrillic.statusRow` strictly when `language == "uk"`) with vertically centered iPad `[абв]` keycap selectors ([`iPadAbvSelectorButton`](file:///Users/love/Documents/Documents%20-%20MacBook%20Air%20-%20%D0%92%D1%96%D1%82%D0%B0/GitHub/Keyboard/QTatar/Demo/Demo/Views/HomeView.swift#L4104-L4135)), and closed-loop vector emblems (**Taraq Tamğa** in national gold `#FFD700` on sky-blue, **Tryzub**, and **Union Jack**) whose resting geometry before and after animation is mathematically identical. The collapsed Statistics view ([`bentoCollapsedStatisticsGrid`](file:///Users/love/Documents/Documents%20-%20MacBook%20Air%20-%20%D0%92%D1%96%D1%82%D0%B0/GitHub/Keyboard/QTatar/Demo/Demo/Views/HomeView.swift#L4752-L4789)) distills user progress into two high-signal Bento tiles—**Words Written** (with unique vocabulary count and subtle cadence sparkline) and **Typing Speed** (paired with a 5-tier human WPM mastery arc `[15, 25, 40, 55, 75]`)—expanding smoothly on tap into a comprehensive lexical observatory. Every alignment, curve, and micro-interaction reflects master-level execution and uncompromising craftsmanship.
