App Review Notes:
1. How to Enable: Open "Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard…" and select "QırımKey" (includes both Crimean Tatar Latin and Cyrillic-to-Latin transliteration keyboards).
2. Privacy & Offline Operation: Both keyboard extensions (`crh.key.keyboard.keyboard` and `crh.key.keyboard.keyboard.cyrillic`) set `RequestsOpenAccess = NO` in `Info.plist` and make zero network requests. Preference synchronization and optional typing statistics between the main app and keyboard extensions occur strictly on-device via the shared App Group container (`group.crh.key.keyboardplus`).
3. System App Settings (`Settings.bundle`): Toggle switches for Word Suggestions, Word Learning, and Typing Statistics & Levels are located in "Settings → Apps → QırımKey" (reachable directly via the "App settings" row on the app's Home screen).
4. Developed by QIRI'M Young NGO (`KYRYM YANH HO`) on the basis of the National Corpus of the Crimean Tatar Language (https://ctcorpus.org).
5. Version Train Note: Version 1.0 is currently live on the App Store, whereas prior TestFlight builds (Builds 1–4) were uploaded under CFBundleShortVersionString = 3.0 (Build 4); therefore this release continues on version train 3.0 (Build ≥ 5).
