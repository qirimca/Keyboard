App Review Notes:
1. How to Enable: Open "Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard…" and select "QırımKey" (includes both Crimean Tatar Latin and Cyrillic-to-Latin transliteration keyboards).
2. Privacy & Offline Operation: Both keyboard extensions (`crh.key.keyboard.keyboard` and `crh.key.keyboard.keyboard.cyrillic`) set `RequestsOpenAccess = YES` in `Info.plist` solely for local on-device App Group (`group.crh.key.keyboardplus`) preference and typing statistics synchronization between `QTatar.app` and the keyboard extensions, and make zero network requests.
3. System App Settings (`Settings.bundle`): Toggle switches for Word Suggestions and Word Learning are located in "Settings → Apps → QırımKey" (reachable directly via the "App settings" row on the app's Home screen), while Typing Statistics is toggled directly in the Statistics card header on the Home screen.
4. Developed by QIRI'M Young NGO (`KYRYM YANH HO`) on the basis of the National Corpus of the Crimean Tatar Language (https://ctcorpus.org).
5. Version Train Note: Version 1.0 is currently live on the App Store, whereas prior TestFlight builds (Builds 1–4) were uploaded under CFBundleShortVersionString = 3.0 (Build 4); therefore this release continues on version train 3.0 (Build ≥ 5).
