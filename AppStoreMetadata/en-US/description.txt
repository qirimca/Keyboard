QırımKey is a modern, intelligent Crimean Tatar keyboard for iPhone and iPad, built on the National Corpus of the Crimean Tatar Language (NCCTL, ctcorpus.org) by QIRI'M Young NGO in collaboration with Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv.

LATIN & CYRILLIC LAYOUTS WITH REAL-TIME TRANSLITERATION
• Dedicated Crimean Tatar Latin layout with direct access to all Crimean Tatar characters (ğ, ñ, ç, ş, ö, ü, ı, â).
• Smart Cyrillic layout with automatic real-time Cyrillic-to-Latin transliteration: type using familiar Cyrillic keys while the keyboard outputs accurate Crimean Tatar Latin script on the fly.
• Optional Ukrainian-adapted Cyrillic layout ("Ukrainian layout", ФІВА) with intuitive mappings (ґ → g, г → ğ, кʼ → q, нʼ → ñ, ї → yi, є → ye/e, і → i, и → ı).

OFFICIAL 2024/2025 ORTHOGRAPHY & SMART PREDICTIONS
• Powered by the National Corpus of the Crimean Tatar Language and compliant with the official 2024/2025 Crimean Tatar Orthography (DESS No. 1722/04-03/25).
• Three-slot suggestion and autocorrection bar supporting hyphenated compound words (gece-kündüz, alış-veriş), automatic merging of question particles (-mı/-mi, e.g. yaptıñ mı → yaptıñmı), and automatic capitalization of Crimean Tatar toponyms (Qırım, Aqmescit, Bağçasaray, Çonğar, Or Qapı).
• Instant single-tap Backspace (⌫) undo of the last space-committed autocorrection.
• Personalized on-device word and bigram learning.

FAST GLIDE TYPING (QIRIMKEY SWIPE V2)
• Smooth continuous gesture typing on iPhone and iPad Floating keyboards across both Latin and Cyrillic layouts.
• Single-tap Backspace (⌫) full-word deletion immediately after swiping a word.

DESIGNED FOR iOS & iPadOS 26/27
• Native Apple Liquid Glass aesthetics with automatic Light and Dark Mode synchronization across all host apps and Spotlight.
• Full iPad keyboard support: Docked, Floating, and Split keyboard modes controlled via the bottom-right "⌄⌨" key callout menu and pinch gesture.
• Daily Crimean Tatar proverbs, greetings, and literary quotes in the top banner before typing begins.
• Built-in interactive testing sandbox for field modes (Text, Search, Email, URL, 123) and on-device typing speed and volume progression levels.

100% PRIVATE & OFFLINE BY DESIGN
• QırımKey runs completely offline on your device (RequestsOpenAccess = NO).
• We never collect, transmit, store, or share anything you type or any personal data.
