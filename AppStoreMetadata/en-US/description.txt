QırımKey is a Crimean Tatar keyboard for iPhone and iPad. It was built on the National Corpus of the Crimean Tatar Language (ctcorpus.org) by the QIRI'M Young community together with Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv, for people who write in Ukrainian Crimea and wherever else the language is spoken.

Two ways to type
The Latin layout puts ğ, ñ, ç, ş, ö, ü, ı and â right on the keys. If you are used to Cyrillic, switch to the Cyrillic layout: you press familiar keys and QırımKey writes Crimean Tatar Latin as you go. There is also an optional Ukrainian-style Cyrillic layout (ґ → g, г → ğ, кʼ → q, нʼ → ñ).

Spelling that follows the rules
Suggestions and autocorrection follow the 2024/2025 orthography (DESS No. 1722/04-03/25): hyphenated words such as gece-kündüz, the question particle (yaptıñ mı → yaptıñmı), and capital letters in names like Qırım, Aqmescit and Bağçasaray. Autocorrection only happens when you press space, and one tap on ⌫ undoes it. The keyboard learns the words you use, on your device only.

Swipe typing
Glide across the keys on iPhone, or on the floating keyboard on iPad, in both layouts. A word you just swiped is deleted with a single ⌫.

On iPhone and iPad
Light and Dark appearance, docked, floating and split keyboards on iPad, and a short Crimean Tatar proverb or greeting above the keys before you start typing. A built-in test field lets you try text, search, email and URL modes and watch your typing speed.

Private by design
QırımKey works offline and never makes a network request. What you type is not collected, stored or shared.
