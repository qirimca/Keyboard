App Review Notes:
1. Note on Locale: Because App Store Connect does not currently provide a dedicated Crimean Tatar (`crh`) store listing locale, the Turkish (`tr`) store localization slot is used to serve our Crimean Tatar (`Qırımtatarca`) community (matching https://ctcorpus.org/index.php/crh/).
2. How to Enable: Open "Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard…" and select "QırımKey".
3. Privacy & Offline Operation: Both keyboard extensions (`crh.key.keyboard.keyboard` and `crh.key.keyboard.keyboard.cyrillic`) use `RequestsOpenAccess = YES` in `Info.plist` strictly for local on-device App Group (`group.crh.key.keyboardplus`) synchronization between `QTatar.app` and the keyboard extensions, and make zero network requests.
4. System App Settings (`Settings.bundle`): Toggle switches for Word Suggestions and Word Learning are in "Settings → Apps → QırımKey", while Statistics is toggled directly in the Statistics card header on the Home screen.
